Privacy Policy

Morgan Knows Ball HQ · last updated November 4, 2026

Morgan Knows Ball HQ ("HQ") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Morgan Knows Ball YouTube channel to manage his own channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What HQ accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, HQ uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • read the owner's channel details and his videos' public statistics;
  • read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers and, if available, revenue);
  • upload videos the owner has approved and set their visibility;
  • read public information about other public YouTube channels and videos.

How the information is used and stored

The information is used only to show the owner his own channel's results and to help him prepare and publish his own videos. It is stored only on the owner's computer, and the Google sign-in token is stored there encrypted. HQ does not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect the channel in HQ at any time, which deletes the stored sign-in token. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Data HQ stored on the owner's computer can be deleted by the owner at any time.

YouTube and Google

HQ uses YouTube API Services. By using HQ you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
